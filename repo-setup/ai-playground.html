Hands-on · Supplier risk

AI supplier risk analysis.

Paste a supplier list with countries and categories. The model flags geographic concentration risk and suggests alternative sourcing regions, with named vendors where it can.

Supplier concentration risk analyser
Your supplier list
Customer type
Mapping geographic concentration…
Make this yourself
The prompt behind this tool
Spotting concentration risk across a supplier list is a good fit for AI. The prompt below does what the tool above does, paste it into any assistant with your own data.
Paste this into any AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot…)
You are a supply-chain risk analyst. I'll give you a list of suppliers, each with a country and the category they supply.

Suppliers:
[paste one per line, e.g. "Acme Corp, Taiwan, Semiconductors"]

Do this:
1. Group the suppliers by country and by category.
2. Flag every country where I rely on two or more suppliers, and any single country that covers a category I can't lose. For each, name the specific risk in one sentence (geopolitical, tariff, logistics, single-event).
3. Give an overall priority, High, Moderate, or Low, with a one-line reason.
4. For each concentration, suggest 2–3 alternative sourcing regions and name a few real example suppliers in each, with a short rationale.

Format: a one-line priority first, then "Risks" as a short list, then "Alternatives" as a short list.
Then make it yours
  1. Country and category are the two fields that matter, include both, even roughly.
  2. Add your own definition of a critical category (“can't run the line without it”) so it weighs the right things.
  3. Follow up with “which of these would take longest to qualify a backup for?” to get a sequencing plan.

The output depends on your inputs and which assistant you use. Treat the first answer as a draft to refine, not a finished deliverable.

The prompt behind this tool
Catching invoice exceptions is pattern-matching, which AI handles well. This prompt reproduces what the tool checks.
Paste this into any AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot…)
You are an accounts-payable reviewer. Below are invoice lines in the format: invoice ID | vendor | amount | note.

Lines:
[paste your lines]
