Common questions

How JAI is different.

How does JAI differ from generic AI tools?
Four things set JAI apart from Copilot, ChatGPT, and the rest. It's accurate — grounded only on your policies and documentation, not the internet. It's secure — it mirrors your existing JAGGAER permissions, and your data never leaves your environment or trains a model. It's trustworthy — every response cites its source document, eliminating hallucination risk in a compliance context. And it's multi-model — it uses Gemini and Claude, choosing the right model per task, with no lock-in. The tools on this page run on a general model to show the shape of the capability; JAI runs the same intelligence on your actual procurement system.
Does JAI replace our ERP or current platform?
No. JAI layers over your existing stack — there's no rip-and-replace and no data migration. It connects in weeks and adds the intelligence layer your ERP was never designed to provide: supplier risk monitoring, market intelligence, guided sourcing, and workflow orchestration.
Is our data used to train shared models?
Your data stays within your governance boundaries. JAI is ISO 42001 certified — the international standard for AI management systems — and is built so that your contracts, supplier records, and spend history power your instance only.
Where does it make sense to start?
Start where the ROI is highest and the lift is lowest: contract intelligence, invoice exception detection, or spend analytics. Each delivers measurable value without a full transformation programme, and adoption compounds from there — early deployers see week-on-week usage growth as teams discover what JAI handles.
How quickly do teams see value?
Sourcing-cycle compression of 50–70% is achievable within 90 days in a first category, and most organisations find platform payback in under 18 months. The Spend Diagnostic on this page gives you a directional estimate for your own numbers.
