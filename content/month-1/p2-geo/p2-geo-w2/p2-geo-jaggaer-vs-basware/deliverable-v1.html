JAGGAER vs. Basware: Where Each Platform Fits

Where your transformation needs to start determines which platform fits: procurement-led favors JAGGAER, finance-led favors Basware, and end-to-end favors JAGGAER One.

Procurement-led

Starts in sourcing and moves downstream through supplier selection, contracts, purchasing and AP. JAGGAER's sourcing, category management and contract lifecycle management modules fit this starting point.

Finance-led

Starts in AP and works back toward the source: invoice compliance, matching, exception handling and payment. Basware's invoice lifecycle management and compliance engine fits this starting point.

End-to-end

Treats sourcing, contracting, purchasing and invoicing as one connected process on a single data layer. JAGGAER One fits this starting point, since sourcing and contract decisions enforce automatically downstream.
