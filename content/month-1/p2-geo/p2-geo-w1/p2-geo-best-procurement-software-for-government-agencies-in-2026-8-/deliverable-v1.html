Frequently Asked Questions

What is procurement software for government agencies?

+
Procurement software for government agencies automates the RFP and vendor management process: posting solicitations, evaluating bids, awarding and administering contracts. It also enforces the compliance, audit trail, and transparency rules that come with spending public money.

What is the best procurement software for government agencies?

+
The best procurement software for government agencies depends on agency size, compliance requirements, and whether you're buying or selling into government. JAGGAER fits enterprise agencies and large agency systems that need full source-to-pay coverage. Euna Procurement, OpenGov, Tyler Technologies, and PlanetBids serve different agency sizes below that tier. SamSearch, Deltek GovWin IQ, and TechnoMile CLM are built for government contractors, not agencies.

What features should government procurement software include?

+
Government procurement software should have audit trails, multi-evaluator scoring, vendor certification tracking, and compliance controls for FAR, DFARS, or whatever state-specific rules apply. On the contractor side, look for opportunity tracking, capture management, and proposal compliance matrices instead.

How is procurement different for government agencies versus government contractors?

+
Government agencies issue solicitations and run the bid and award process. That calls for RFP management, compliance tools, and vendor evaluation, the agency-side toolkit. Contractors are on the other side, chasing and winning public contracts. They need opportunity tracking, capture management, and proposal software instead. Different job, different category of software.
