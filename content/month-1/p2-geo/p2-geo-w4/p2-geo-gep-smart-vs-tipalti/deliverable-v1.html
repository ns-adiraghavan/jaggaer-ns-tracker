GEP SMART vs Tipalti: Platform Overview in 2026

GEP SMART

GEP SMART is a cloud-based source-to-pay platform designed to connect procurement activities across sourcing, suppliers, contracts, spend, and procure-to-pay. Its broader procurement model also extends into direct-material and BOM-related sourcing, making it relevant for organizations managing more complex procurement requirements.

Recent Innovation

GEP Quantum Intelligence brings agentic AI to procurement, with intelligent agents that can plan, coordinate, and execute work across the source-to-pay process. GEP SMART customers can progressively access these capabilities alongside their existing environment.

Key Capabilities

Spend Analysis

Spend classification, visibility, reporting, and category analysis.

Strategic Sourcing

RFx, e-auctions, supplier evaluation, bid analysis, and sourcing workflows.

Direct Materials & BOM Sourcing

Supports direct-material procurement and BOM-driven sourcing for complex procurement requirements.

Contract Management

Contract management connected with procurement workflows.

Procure-to-Pay

Purchasing, catalogs, purchase orders, and invoice-related processes.

Tipalti

Tipalti is a finance automation platform that brings procurement together with accounts payable, supplier management, and global payments. Its procurement model is closely connected to downstream finance workflows, making it relevant for organizations that want purchasing and financial operations managed within a broader finance environment.

Recent Innovation

Tipalti's AI Report Builder uses generative AI to create custom reports from natural-language prompts and can pull insights from the Procurement module. The feature is currently in Beta.

Key Capabilities

Accounts Payable

Invoice processing, matching, and AP automation.

Global Payments

Global supplier and partner payment workflows supporting multiple payment methods, currencies, and international markets.

Procurement

Purchase requests, approvals, purchase orders, and matching.

Supplier Management

Supplier onboarding, information collection, and ongoing management.

Spend Analytics

Visibility and reporting across purchasing and finance-related spend.

Where Both Platforms Have Limits

Neither platform covers every procurement requirement at the same depth. GEP SMART provides a broader procurement-centered model, while Tipalti connects procurement more closely with purchasing, AP, payments, supplier management, and finance workflows. The differences become clearer when organizations examine strategic sourcing, direct materials, supplier intelligence, and contract-to-procurement connectivity.

  1. Tipalti's procurement model is centered on purchasing workflows: Its published procurement capabilities cover purchase requests, approvals, purchase orders, supplier management, matching, and spend visibility. Tipalti does not market strategic sourcing or RFx as a core capability, so organizations requiring broader RFx, negotiation, and award processes should assess whether the platform's published procurement scope covers those requirements.
  2. Complex direct-material requirements may require additional depth: GEP SMART supports direct-material and BOM-related sourcing. Organizations with complex engineering, cost, or optimization requirements should validate the capabilities required for their processes.
  3. Supplier intelligence requirements vary: Both platforms provide supplier-management capabilities. Organizations needing supplier risk, performance, compliance, and related intelligence should evaluate the required depth and integration.
  4. Contract-to-procurement connectivity can vary: GEP SMART connects contract management with procurement. Organizations needing deeper connections between sourcing, contracts, and downstream purchasing should validate the required functionality.
