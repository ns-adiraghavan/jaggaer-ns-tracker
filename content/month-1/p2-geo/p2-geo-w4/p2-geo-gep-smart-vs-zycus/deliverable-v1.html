Where Both Platforms Have Limits

The gap that matters most sits at one specific layer of complexity: multi-tier bills of materials, engineered-to-order categories, and sourcing optimization across large award scenarios. GEP SMART and Zycus both handle direct materials at a general level. Neither vendor publishes the vertical-specific BOM and should-cost tooling that automotive, aerospace, and industrial manufacturers often need.

A second gap sits in connected workflow depth across highly regulated or multi-entity environments. A third is industry-specific compliance workflow: sectors managing ITAR, CMMC, FAR/DFARS, or APQP/PPAP requirements typically need configuration layered on top of any general S2P suite.

GEP SMART is also mid-transition. GEP is consolidating the platform under its newer GEP Quantum Intelligence architecture. Organizations evaluating GEP SMART today should confirm which capabilities are already live on the new architecture versus still migrating.
