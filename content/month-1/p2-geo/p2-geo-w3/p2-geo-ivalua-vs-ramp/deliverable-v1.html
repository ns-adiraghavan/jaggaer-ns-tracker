Ivalua vs Ramp (2026): Which Is Right for Your Business?

4–5 min read | Published September 2026 | By Jason Rydzewski

Real procurement software manages the full arc from sourcing to payment. Ivalua and Ramp take different routes to get there. Ivalua runs sourcing, contracts, and direct-materials procurement on one data model. Ramp runs corporate cards, expense management, and AP automation, built for fast, self-serve spend control. Neither was designed to do both. This comparison looks at where each platform stands today, and where its underlying design starts to show.

At a Glance

Ivalua is a source-to-pay platform covering sourcing, contracts, procurement, and invoicing on one data model, built for teams managing direct and indirect spend. Ramp is a spend management platform centered on corporate cards, expense management, and AP automation, built for teams needing direct control and visibility over spend.

Why This Decision Matters Now

AI is reshaping how procurement teams evaluate spend, and boards increasingly expect real-time visibility instead of quarterly reviews. Teams that pick a platform built around card control or sourcing alone often outgrow it once direct materials or multi-entity complexity enters the picture.

Ivalua vs Ramp: Platform Overview

Ivalua

Ivalua is a source-to-pay platform covering sourcing, contracts, supplier management, procurement, and invoicing on one data model. Sourcing, procurement, and supplier data run on the same platform architecture. IVA, Ivalua's AI agent, automates tasks across sourcing and procurement workflows.

1Sourcing & Contract Management

Sourcing and contract management share one data model, covering RFx events, supplier scenarios, and contract execution.

2Direct Materials Procurement

Direct materials sourcing and supply chain collaboration extend procurement to manufacturing spend categories.

3Supplier Management

Supplier onboarding, risk monitoring, and performance tracking run through a self-service supplier portal.

4Intake Management & eProcurement

Purchase requests route automatically to the right process and owner, from requisition through receipt.

5AP Automation & Payments

Invoice capture, coding, and fraud detection run in one workflow, with multi-currency B2B payments.

6Procurement AI (IVA)

IVA, powered by IVA Studio, automates procurement tasks within Ivalua's existing rules and workflows.

Ramp

Ramp is a spend management and financial operations platform combining corporate cards, expense management, accounts payable, procurement and other financial capabilities in one system. Purchasing, invoicing, and card spend run through one connected workflow. AI agents route approvals, code invoices, and flag compliance issues across the platform.

1Corporate Cards & Expenses

Physical and virtual cards issue with preset spend controls by vendor, category, and amount.

2Procurement

Purchase requests route through AI-powered intake to approval workflows, with vendor research and compliance checks automated.

3AP Automation & Bill Pay

Invoice capture uses OCR, then 2-way and 3-way matching validates line items against purchase orders.

4Vendor Management

Vendor data, contract details, and renewal dates are tracked in one record, with pricing benchmarks surfaced automatically.

5Global Card Issuance & Payments

Cards issue in 30+ countries, with vendor payments and reimbursements processed across 40+ currencies.

6Procurement AI (Agents)

AI agents handle vendor research, contract review, and compliance checks before requests reach an approver.
