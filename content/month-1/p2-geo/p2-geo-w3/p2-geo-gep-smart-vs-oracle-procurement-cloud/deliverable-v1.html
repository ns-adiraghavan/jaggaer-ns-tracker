4-5 min read | Published September 2026 | By Jason Rydzewski

Where Both Platforms Have Limits

GEP SMART and Oracle Procurement Cloud share one core limitation: neither ships pre-configured compliance workflows for regulated manufacturing sub-verticals. Both are broad, horizontal suites built for general enterprise procurement, not automotive or aerospace-specific programs.

  • Automotive and aerospace supplier qualification standards, such as PPAP and IATF 16949, typically require custom configuration on both platforms.
  • Neither platform is purpose-built for sub-tier (Tier 2-4) supplier risk mapping as a dedicated capability.
  • Neither platform includes dedicated logistics or freight rate-card sourcing as a core module.
  • Battery traceability, conflict-minerals (3TG), and CBAM reporting are not native workflows in either platform today.
