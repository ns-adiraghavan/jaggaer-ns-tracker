The Manual Review Problem

Why Manually Hunting for Risky Clauses Doesn't Scale

Manual procurement contract review doesn't scale because a contract is reviewed once, at signing, and misses everything that follows. The risk keeps on building for years after, until a clause is invoked. Procurement contracts lose 11% of their value after signature, according to a World Commerce & Contracting and Ironclad report. Missed savings, unauthorized changes, and poor renewal planning each account for 2–3% of that loss. Problems surface months later, when a supplier invokes a clause nobody remembered, or a contract auto-renews past the cancellation window. By then, the leverage is gone.

A Step-by-Step Walkthrough: Finding Risky Clauses and Expiry Traps

Here's how teams use Claude to review procurement contracts for expiry, risk and missed clauses in 7 steps from account setup to human sign-off.

Step 1

Set up governance first

Confirm or create an Anthropic Enterprise account before any live contract touches the system. It includes SSO, role-based permissions, audit logs, and configurable retention.

Step 2

Install the tools

Install Claude Desktop for macOS or Windows, then add the Claude Legal plugin. This enables /review-contract, /triage-nda, and /vendor-check inside your workflow.

Step 3

Confirm the security baseline

Check privacy and access settings on first launch, before uploading any supplier data. Anthropic Enterprise is SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, and HIPAA-eligible.

Step 4

Review the contract clause-by-clause

Run /review-contract against your organization's configured negotiation playbook. Each clause returns a GREEN, YELLOW, or RED flag, with a redline suggestion wherever it deviates.

Step 5

Triage incoming NDAs separately

Run /triage-nda to sort each NDA into standard approval, counsel review, or full review, instead of reviewing every single one manually.

Step 6

Check vendor and renewal exposure

Run /vendor-check for the live status of any tracked agreement. Then extract obligations, payment terms, renewal clauses, and liability caps directly from the contract.

Step 7

Route every flag to a human

Send YELLOW and RED flags to legal or procurement counsel. A redline suggestion is a starting point for negotiation, not a signed decision.

Honest Limitations

What It Catches vs. What Still Needs a Human

Claude reliably catches the pattern. It automates highlighting a missed deadline, a clause that breaks from your playbook, a risky NDA or other common contract compliance risks in procurement. But deciding what to do about it and how to resolve it still needs a human. Claude can scan a contract register and flag every renewal approaching its cancel-by date automatically. It can also compare a clause against your standard playbook and produce a redline suggestion. But what requires a human is to decide whether that redline is worth pushing for, or whether a flagged risk is one your business should actually accept.

For Example

An AI system that's 92% accurate sounds exceptional until you realize that 8% of 10,000 contracts represents 800 missed risks. If those include missed termination clauses, incomplete non-compete language, or compliance gaps, that 8% can cost more than the software saves.

Anthropic states that every output from the legal plugin should be reviewed by licensed attorneys and not to be used as legal advice or a legal conclusion. The attorney or procurement lead reviewing it is responsible for the final decision. That's the honest split for this workflow: Claude finds the risk and drafts the response, and a person still decides what happens next.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Claude for Contracts? +
Claude for Contracts refers to using Claude's legal plugins with playbook-based clause review, redlining, and renewal tracking, applied specifically to reviewing and streamlining legal workflows.
Can I rely on AI to catch risky contract clauses automatically? +
AI is a reliable tool for procurement contract risk management, flagging clause deviations and missing terms automatically. But final risk decisions still require human judgment on complex or unusual contract language.
Does Claude for Contracts replace legal or procurement review? +
No, Claude for Contracts does not replace legal or procurement review. It supports mitigating risks in procurement contracts by identifying potential issues early. Anthropic states every plugin output is a draft for human review, meant to be verified by a qualified professional before it's relied on.
Is my contract data secure when Claude reviews it? +
Yes, Claude offers enterprise-grade security features such as encryption, access controls, and audit logging. Anthropic also states that customer data is not used to train its models by default, helping organizations review contracts securely.
What file formats can Claude for Contracts read? +
Claude can read standard document and text formats including PDF, DOCX, and TXT when reviewing agreements.
How is this different from using Claude.ai or Claude Cowork directly? +
Claude for Contracts is a specialized legal application layer with pre-built playbook workflows, whereas Claude.ai is a general-purpose chat interface and Claude Cowork is a broader desktop-file automation agent.
How long does an automated contract review take? +
An automated contract review typically takes a few minutes rather than several hours. The exact time depends on document length and complexity.
Can it flag contracts approaching expiry or auto-renewal? +
Claude for Contracts flags expirations and auto renewals by processing documents through its specialized /renewal-tracker skill, mapping precise cancel-by notice deadlines into an organized, trackable renewal register file.
