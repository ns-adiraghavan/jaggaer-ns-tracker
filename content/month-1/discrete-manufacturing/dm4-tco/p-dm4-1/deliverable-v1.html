Structure, Not Vendor

Why This Is a Contract-Structure Problem, Not a Vendor Problem

Hidden costs in a procurement platform contract are a structure problem because the same costly terms exist across every vendor's paperwork. Usage tiers, escalators, and exit clauses aren't unique to one platform. They surface only when a buyer fails to negotiate against them upfront. NPI's research found companies overpay on 89% of their IT purchases. That gap reflects a missing benchmark. Any platform can be sold this way if the buyer doesn't ask the right questions first. Comparing vendor reputations solves the wrong problem. Comparing contract structures solves the actual one. This comparison helps you find the true cost of an S2P platform for your manufacturing operations.

Before You Sign

Questions to Ask Before You Sign (or Renew) a Contract in 2026

These seven questions expose the hidden costs in your procurement platform before you sign and bring this list into every renewal or RFP conversation.

Bring This List to Your Next Vendor Call
  • What happens to pricing at renewal, and is any increase capped?
  • Is implementation billed separately from the license, and what's included?
  • What's the true cost per additional module or user tier?
  • Is data migration included, or billed as a separate line item?
  • What's the cancellation-notice window, and does the contract auto-renew?
  • What format does our data export in if we exit the contract?
  • What triggers a support-tier upgrade, and what does it cost?

Answering these questions gives you the true cost of an S2P platform for manufacturing before you sign any contract.

Frequently Asked Questions

What hidden costs should I look for in a procurement platform contract? +
The most common hidden costs are implementation fees, per-module pricing escalation, integration and API charges, data migration, support-tier upsells, auto-renewal clauses, and exit or data-portability fees.
Why do procurement software implementations often cost more than quoted? +
Procurement software implementations often cost more than quoted due to hidden customization fees, unexpected data migration hurdles, and ongoing vendor subscription add-ons. IT leaders report an average 18% cost overrun on platform migrations.
What's a fair way to structure per-user or per-module pricing? +
A fair pricing structure locks per-module rates for the full contract term and caps user-tier increases at renewal, preventing costs from scaling faster than adoption. Getting this right is central to controlling the true cost of an S2P platform in manufacturing environments.
What should I ask about data migration before signing? +
Ask whether data migration is included in the base license or billed separately and request a fixed-fee quote before signing.
How do auto-renewal clauses affect procurement software costs? +
Auto-renewal clauses lock companies into unwanted licenses, missing opportunities to negotiate lower market rates, and triggering automatic price hikes or hidden fee additions.
How do procurement software contract costs compare to other SaaS categories, like cloud or contact center vendors? +
Procurement platforms follow the same hidden-cost pattern as cloud and contact-center software. SaaS costs from major vendors rose 10–20% in 2025, nearly four times faster than IT budget growth.
What does vendor lock-in actually cost if I want to switch platforms later? +
Vendor lock-in costs can be very high. They include costs from data re-migration, integration rebuilds, retraining staff, and running parallel systems during transition.
