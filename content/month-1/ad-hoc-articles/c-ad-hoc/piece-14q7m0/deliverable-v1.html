Glossary

What is Touchless PO Processing Rate?

The percentage of purchase orders that move from creation to issuance without any manual intervention.

What is PO Issuance Time?

The time it takes a purchase order to move from approval to being sent to a supplier.

What is Requisition-to-PO Cycle Time?

The time it takes a submitted requisition to become an approved purchase order.

What is Invoice Processing Cycle Time?

The time it takes an invoice to move from receipt to payment-ready status.

What is Touchless Invoice Processing Rate?

The share of invoices processed without manual intervention, from capture through matching.

What is Invoice Extraction Accuracy?

How correctly a system reads and captures data from an invoice, typically using AI-OCR.

What is Invoice Approval Cycle Time?

How long an invoice takes to move through the approval workflow after processing.
