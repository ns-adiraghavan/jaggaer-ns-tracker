Start where the request starts

What is AI intake in procurement?

Intake AI captures a purchase request in plain language, fills the structured fields and checks the request before a buyer sees it.

Elsewhere, AI intake also means patient intake or a gate for new AI projects. In procurement, it means the purchase request and everything procurement needs to act on it.

AI intake sits at the start of intake management in procurement. The system checks before submission, and people still approve.

Capture. Check. Route.

How AI intake works

The AI intake process runs in 4 steps: describe, pre-fill, check and route.

1
Describe the need

The requester types the need or uploads a quote. Conversational intake AI asks follow-up questions only where an answer is missing.

2
Pre-fill the form

AI pre-fill forms for intake complete category, quantity, supplier, cost center and delivery date from the request. The requester confirms or corrects each field.

3
Check against policy

The system checks catalogs, contracts, budget and approval thresholds, then flags any rule the request breaks, with the source.

4
Route to the right channel

Catalog items become a requisition and new needs go to sourcing. Anything unusual or above threshold goes to a person.

Ask every vendor which of the 4 steps its AI performs and which a person still owns. Jaggaer’s product page states that JAGGAER AI (JAI) takes a request from requisition intake to compliant purchase order (PO) submission. A person approves at every checkpoint.

Stop making requesters guess

AI intake vs traditional intake forms

An AI intake form fills itself from the request, while a traditional form waits for the requester to fill it.

What changesTraditional intake formAI intake, reading your policies and contracts
Who knows the policyThe requester, from memory or a PDFThe assistant, from your policies and contracts
Missing informationA buyer finds it after submissionThe assistant asks for it before submission
RoutingThe requester’s form choice sets itCatalog, contract, threshold and budget rules set it
ExceptionsApprovers find them lateThe assistant flags them at request, with the reason
What procurement seesFree text and attachmentsStructured fields, a stated reason and a route

Replace the guessing, not the form. Teams that replace intake forms with AI still need structured fields for approvers, enterprise resource planning (ERP) systems and spend reports. Let AI complete them; the requester confirms.

What to look for in an AI intake assistant

Judge an AI intake assistant on what it checks, what it logs and when it stops for a person. A fluent chat proves none of these.

Deloitte’s 2026 State of AI in the Enterprise names 3 governance capabilities most organizations lack for agentic AI. They are decision boundaries, real-time monitoring and audit trails. Deloitte surveyed 3,235 IT and business leaders in 24 countries, August to September 2025.

Run these 6 tests in every demo. Tests 3 to 5 adapt Deloitte’s governance examples to intake.

1

Grounded answers

The assistant checks your policies, contracts and catalogs and cites the source.

Test it: ask a policy question with a known threshold. Check the citation.

2

Pre-fill accuracy

Category, supplier, quantity and cost center come from the request or quote.

Test it: submit 10 real requests. Count the fields you correct.

3

Decision boundaries

You define which requests need a person’s approval.

Test it: set a value threshold, then submit a request just above it.

4

Real-time monitoring

Overrides and reroutes show up while the pilot runs.

Test it: ask to see last week’s overrides in the demo tenant.

5

Audit trail

Every suggestion, edit and approval carries a time, user and source.

Test it: trace a request from the first prompt to the purchase order.

6

Handoff into execution

The request becomes a requisition, PO or sourcing event without retyping.

Test it: follow a non-catalog request to a PO. Count the re-keyed fields.

Ask which policies, contracts and catalogs the assistant reads, and who keeps each current.

Governance and risk in AI intake

Govern AI intake with named approvers, logged decisions and clear limits on what routes without review. The risks: a wrong supplier pre-filled, a request misrouted or a policy answer without a source.

Deloitte’s same survey, published in 2026, found 74% expect their companies to use AI agents at least moderately by 2027. But only 21% had a mature agentic AI governance model in that 2026 report. Tests 3 to 5 rest on that result.

Check that any platform’s embedded intelligence and AI cites its source, respects user permissions and logs every suggestion.

How to pilot AI intake

Pilot AI intake on 1 or 2 high-volume, lower-risk request types, with a baseline and exit criteria agreed before go-live. Decide what AI intake automation takes over first: answers, pre-fill or routing.

Run the pilot in 5 steps:

1
Pick the request types

Choose frequent requests with clear policy, not capital or regulated buys.

2
Baseline the current process

Measure for 4 weeks: requests returned, time to approved requisition, misroutes and off-contract share.

3
Set the rules before configuration

Agree thresholds, what always goes to a person and what counts as success.

4
Measure the process, not the user

Track team-level cycle time and rework, not individual time saved.

5
Decide: scale, adjust or stop

Compare results with the baseline and scale only what improved.

Gartner found generative AI’s individual productivity gains decline significantly at team output level, across 101 CPOs surveyed in January and February 2026.

Straight answers

Frequently asked questions

What is AI intake in procurement? +
AI intake in procurement captures a purchase request in plain language and completes the structured fields. It checks policy, contracts and budget, then routes the request to a buying channel or a person.
What is an AI intake process, and how is it different from an AI use case intake process? +
An AI intake process in procurement handles purchase requests in 4 steps: describe, pre-fill, check and route. An AI use case intake process reviews proposed AI projects before teams build or buy them.
Can AI fill out a procurement intake form for the requester? +
AI pre-fills category, quantity, supplier and cost center from a request or quote. The requester confirms each field, and a buyer reviews anything above threshold.
Can AI replace intake forms entirely? +
AI intake can replace the guessing on intake forms, not the structured record. Approvers, enterprise resource planning (ERP) systems and spend reports still need consistent fields.
What does an AI intake assistant do? +
An AI intake assistant asks follow-up questions, pre-fills the request, checks policy and routes it. A good one cites its source and hands high-value requests to a person.
Is conversational intake the same as a chatbot? +
Conversational intake differs from a general chatbot because it ends in a structured, policy-checked request that procurement can act on.
What data does AI intake need to route requests correctly? +
AI intake needs current catalogs, contracts, preferred suppliers, approval thresholds and budget data. Give each data source a named owner.
