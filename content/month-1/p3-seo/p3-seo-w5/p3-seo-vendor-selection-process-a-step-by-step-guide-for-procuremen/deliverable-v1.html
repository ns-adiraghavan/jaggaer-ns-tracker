Definition

What Is Vendor Selection? (And How It's Different from Sourcing or Supplier Management)

Vendor selection is the process of choosing which supplier receives a contract, based on defined requirements, weighted criteria and verification. It differs from sourcing in scope: sourcing is the broader process, and vendor selection is one stage within it. It differs from supplier management in timing: vendor selection ends at contract signing, and supplier management starts there. Many teams use suppliers and vendors interchangeably, but our guide to vendor vs supplier explains where they differ.

Supplier management covers contract performance, supplier relationship management and periodic re-evaluation. Vendor evaluation is the assessment of a supplier against set criteria, both before selection and when re-evaluating existing suppliers. ISO 9001 clause 8.4.1 makes this a formal requirement for certified organizations.

Step by Step

The 7 Steps in the Vendor Selection Process

The vendor selection process runs in seven steps, moving from internal planning to a signed contract. Teams first identify needs and requirements, research and screen vendors, and set weighted selection criteria. They then issue an RFP or RFQ and evaluate proposals against the scorecard. Finally, they verify compliance and financial stability, then negotiate and select.

  1. 01
    Step 1 of 7

    Identify needs & requirements

    Document your needs, budget, timeline and the supplier's criticality, then get sign-off from all users and budget holders. Separate must-haves from nice-to-haves and base the budget on past spend data. Then score profit impact and supply risk, as in the Kraljic matrix. Supply risk is high when few suppliers can deliver or switching is hard. A purchase high on both counts is strategic.

    ✓
    Output

    Signed-off requirements and a risk tier (low, medium or strategic).

  2. 02
    Step 2 of 7

    Vendor Screening Process

    The vendor screening process narrows the market to a shortlist before a full RFP goes out. Start with a long list from your supplier base, supplier networks, referrals and industry directories. JAGGAER's network offers over 13 million pre-validated suppliers. Then send a pre-qualification questionnaire or RFI that tests each minimum requirement. EU procurement law offers a useful model: legal suitability, financial standing, and technical and professional ability. Vendors that fail any requirement are excluded, whatever their price.

    EU model Legal suitability Financial standing Technical and professional ability
    ✓
    Output

    a shortlist of 3–6 vendors.

  3. 03
    Step 3 of 7

    Set weighted selection criteria

    Decide what disqualifies a vendor and how the rest are scored before any vendor responds. Knockouts are pass/fail: insurance, required certifications, sanctions screening and security standards such as ISO 27001. Weight the remaining criteria, such as quality, delivery, risk, service and total cost of ownership, to total 100%. Public buyers have no choice: US FAR 15.304 and EU Directive 2014/24/EU require criteria and their relative importance disclosed upfront.

    Knockouts Insurance Required certifications Sanctions screening ISO 27001
    ✓
    Output

    A locked scoring model, ready for the RFP.

  4. 04
    Step 4 of 7

    Issue an RFP or RFQ

    Send an RFP when the solution is complex, and an RFQ when the specification is fixed and price is the main variable. Our complete comparison of RFP vs RFQ vs RFI shows where each request fits. Give every vendor the same template, deadline and evaluation criteria. Collect technical and price responses separately, the "two-envelope" method used in World Bank and many EU public tenders. Share every answer to a vendor's question with all bidders; FAR 15.201 requires this to prevent unfair advantage.

    ✓
    Output

    Comparable proposals, with pricing sealed until quality is scored.

  5. 05
    Step 5 of 7

    Evaluate proposals against the scorecard

    The vendor evaluation process scores every shortlisted proposal against the weighted scorecard, criterion by criterion. At least two evaluators should score the non-price criteria independently, before anyone sees pricing. They then discuss large gaps between their scores and agree on a consensus score. Demos and interviews belong inside this step, with every vendor following the same script and scored against the same criteria. Ask vendors to confirm demo claims in writing, because US federal guidance notes oral presentations cannot be incorporated into a contract by reference.

    ✓
    Output

    a ranked list with a written rationale.

  6. 06
    Step 6 of 7

    Ensuring Compliance During Vendor Selection

    Due diligence verifies a finalist's proposal claims before you sign. Check your top one or two vendors in five areas:

    References

    Ask two or three similar customers how the vendor handled delays and defects.

    Financial health

    Review audited accounts for weak cash flow, high debt or dependence on one customer.

    Sanctions

    Screen the company and its owners against lists such as OFAC's SDN list.

    Cybersecurity

    Request ISO 27001 or SOC 2 evidence. Third parties were involved in 48% of breaches in the 2026 Verizon DBIR.

    ESG

    Check labour, human rights and environmental risks. Large companies face EU supplier due diligence duties from 26 July 2029, under the CSDDD.

    ✓
    Output

    A documented risk assessment for each finalist.

  7. 07
    Step 7 of 7

    Negotiate and Select

    Finish in four moves: negotiate, award, debrief losing bidders and onboard the winner.

    Negotiate

    Turn the proposal into SLAs and KPIs with measurable targets, plus remedies if they are missed. Include conditions from due diligence.

    Award

    Re-check exclusion lists just before signing. US federal contracting officers must review SAM.gov again immediately before award.

    Debrief

    Explain to each unsuccessful bidder why it lost. Federal agencies must debrief bidders who ask within 3 days of the award notice (FAR 15.506).

    Onboard

    Collect tax, banking and insurance details, then create the supplier record. Network suppliers onboard faster because they already work with JAGGAER customers.

    ✓
    Output

    A signed contract and an active supplier record.

Criteria

Vendor Selection Criteria That Hold Up to Scrutiny

The core vendor selection criteria are quality, delivery reliability, total cost of ownership, financial stability, and compliance and risk. These criteria for selecting a vendor also serve as vendor evaluation criteria when suppliers are re-evaluated. For direct materials and regulated industries, weight compliance and supply continuity above price. Since February 2, 2026, the FDA's QMSR has required medical device makers to set supplier criteria proportionate to product risk.

Core criteria Quality Delivery reliability Total cost of ownership Financial stability Compliance and risk
Selecting a Software Vendor?

In a software vendor selection process, security, integration, uptime and roadmap carry extra weight. Ask for ISO/IEC 27001 or SOC 2 Type II evidence, and test API fit against your systems. Require uptime SLAs with measurable targets, as DORA requires in EU financial firms' ICT contracts. Software vendor selection should also review the product roadmap, since the product changes after signing.

Scorecard

Building a Vendor Scorecard (With a Reusable Selection Checklist)

A vendor scorecard turns every proposal into one comparable number: score each criterion, multiply it by its weight, then add the results. Assign the weights first, totaling 100%, and lock them before the RFP is issued. Give the most weight to the criteria where failure would cost the most; for regulated buyers, that is usually compliance and risk. Score each non-price criterion on a fixed scale, such as 0–5, with a written description of what each score means. Use the scorecard as part of your vendor selection checklist to keep evaluation criteria consistent across proposals.

A common public-sector method gives the lowest bid full marks and scales the others by lowest bid ÷ this bid × maximum points. Keep minimum requirements as pass/fail, outside the weighted score. In the example below, Vendor A is cheaper, but Vendor B wins on compliance and continuity.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is vendor evaluation the same thing as vendor selection?+
No, vendor evaluation is not the same as vendor selection. Evaluation assesses a supplier against set criteria, while selection is the decision to award the contract. ISO 9001 lists them as separate steps.
What are the four stages of supplier selection?+
The four stages of supplier selection are problem definition, formulation of criteria, supplier qualification and final choice. This framework comes from de Boer, Labro and Morlacchi (2001). Seven-step processes split the same stages into more operational tasks.
What are the 7 steps of the procurement process?+
The 7 procurement steps are: identify the need, raise a requisition, select a supplier, issue a purchase order, receive goods, process the invoice and pay. Vendor selection is only the third step.
What are the best practices for vendor selection decision-making?+
The best practices for vendor selection decision-making are fixing criteria and weights before bids arrive, scoring quality before prices open, and keeping evaluation records. EU procurement law requires advance weighting, and ISO 9001 requires retained evaluation records.
How many vendors should you shortlist before sending an RFP?+
You should shortlist at least three vendors before sending an RFP. US federal rules suggest soliciting at least three sources for simplified purchases, while EU restricted procedures require at least five candidates.
What happens if no vendor clears your scorecard threshold?+
If no vendor clears your scorecard threshold, no contract should be awarded. Check whether the requirements were realistic, revise the specification and re-tender. EU public buyers may then negotiate directly only if the original contract conditions stay substantially unchanged.
Should you use an RFP or an RFQ for vendor selection?+
You should use an RFP when vendors must propose a solution, and an RFQ when the specification is fixed and price decides. Under US federal rules, RFP processes allow best-value trade-offs, while quotations are not binding offers.
Who should be involved in making the final vendor decision?+
The final vendor decision should involve procurement, budget holders, end users, and legal, finance, IT or quality specialists where relevant. Anyone who can influence the outcome should declare conflicts of interest, which EU procurement law requires.
