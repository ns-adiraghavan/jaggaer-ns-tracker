Definition

What is requisition management software?

Requisition management software is the system procurement uses to capture, validate, route, and approve purchase requests before they become purchase orders. It usually sits inside eProcurement software or a broader Procure-to-Pay suite rather than standing alone. Purchase requisition software belongs to the same category; job requisition tools used in HR recruiting do not.

The meaningful difference between products is scope, not the label. Some requisition management software only digitizes the request form and the approval chain. Others connect each request to catalogs, contracts, budgets, supplier records, and the ERP. The second group governs more spend, because controls apply before money is committed rather than after.

Workflow

What does requisition management software do?

Requisition management software turns an unstructured request into a compliant, correctly coded, budget-checked, and approved order. On a mature platform, six activities run as one workflow.

  1. 01

    Intake

    Every need enters through one front door, whether it starts as a catalog search, a form, or a free-text description. Good intake also recognizes requests that are not simple purchases, such as onboarding a new supplier, and routes them to the right process.

    Entry pointsCatalog searchFormFree-text
  2. 02

    Guided buying

    Guided buying steers requesters to the right channel before they pick a supplier: contracted catalogs, punchouts, internal inventory, or a sourcing request. Occasional requesters benefit most, because they rarely know the buying policy.

    ChannelsContracted catalogsPunchoutsInternal inventorySourcing request
  3. 03

    Coding and classification

    The requisition is where spend receives its commodity code, cost center, and GL account. Errors made here flow into the PO, the invoice match, and every spend report built on that data.

    For example

    In higher education, research labs often request specialist supplies outside the catalog. Category-specific forms in requisition management software capture the specification and route the request to the right approver.

    Errors flow intoPO→Invoice match→Spend reports
  4. 04

    Budget check and approval

    The system confirms available funds, then routes approval by value, category, cost center, or risk. Some platforms also commit budget at requisition, so finance sees the obligation before a PO exists.

    Routing rulesValueCategoryCost centerRisk
  5. 05

    Contract compliance

    Negotiated prices, preferred suppliers, and approval thresholds apply automatically. Requesters no longer need to know which agreement covers which purchase.

  6. 06

    PO creation and ERP sync

    The approved requisition becomes a purchase order without re-keying, and commitments, receipts, and supplier data stay consistent with the ERP. See purchase order automation for the downstream steps.

    Stays in syncCommitmentsReceiptsSupplier data

The payoff from connecting these steps is speed. The Hackett Group's 2025 research found that top-quartile procurement organizations run requisition-to-PO cycle times 58% shorter than their peers.

Selection

How to choose requisition management software

Choose requisition management software by measuring your own process first, then testing finalists against your hardest real requests. Vendor-scripted demos show the standard path. Your exceptions show the fit.

  1. 01

    Baseline the process

    Measure requisition-to-PO cycle time, cost per PO, off-contract spend, and the share of requests returned for rework.

    MeasureCycle timeCost per POOff-contract spendRework rate
  2. 02

    Define the scope

    Decide whether you need standalone requisition management software, full eProcurement, or end-to-end Procure-to-Pay. Scope determines which integrations and stakeholders join the evaluation.

  3. 03

    Script the demos

    Give every finalist the same four scenarios: a non-catalog services request, a budget overrun, a delegated multi-level approval, and a new-supplier request.

    ScenariosNon-catalog services requestBudget overrunDelegated multi-level approvalNew-supplier request
  4. 04

    Test with real requesters

    Time how long an occasional requester takes to submit a compliant request. Requesters bypass tools that feel slower than email.

  5. 05

    Check governance

    Review audit trails, separation of duties, AI controls, and who can change approval rules.

  6. 06

    Count the ongoing effort

    Ask who maintains catalogs, punchouts, and ERP connectors after go-live.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How is requisition management software different from eProcurement software?

+
Requisition management software covers intake, approvals and PO conversion. eProcurement software adds catalogs, supplier enablement, receiving, and invoicing around it.

What is the difference between a purchase requisition and a purchase order?

+
A purchase requisition is an internal request to buy. A purchase order is the external commitment to a supplier, created from the approved requisition.

Can requisition management software handle services purchases?

+
Yes, if the platform supports configurable forms. Look for forms that capture quotes, statements of work, and supplier details, with approval routing by category and value.

Should requisition management software check or commit budget?

+
A check confirms funds are available. A commitment reserves them, so finance sees the obligation before the PO is issued. Organizations with tight fund control, such as public sector bodies, often require commitment.

Can requisition approvals be fully automated?

+
Low-risk requisitions can be approved automatically under rules the procurement team sets. High-value, unusual, or off-contract requests should still reach a human approver, with every automated decision logged.
