Source to Contract vs Source to Pay vs Procure to Pay: Key Differences

About 8 min read | By Visnja Radosavljevic | Published December 2024 | Updated October 2026

Source to contract (S2C) is the upstream half of source to pay (S2P); procure to pay (P2P) is the downstream half. S2C decides who you buy from and on what terms. P2P carries out the purchase under those terms, from requisition to payment. S2P joins both halves into a single lifecycle.

What is source to contract (S2C)?

Source to contract (S2C) covers procurement from spend analysis to a signed supplier contract, then manages that contract through renewal. It sets who you buy from and on what terms. Buying under that contract belongs to P2P.

According to The Hackett Group (September 2026), contract lifecycle management is the final concrete step of S2C.

The 4 activity areas of S2C

S2C has 4 activity areas: spend management, category management, strategic sourcing and contract management.

Spend management.Spend analytics shows where your money goes and with which suppliers. Gartner (page updated September 2025) defines these tools as cleansing, normalizing, enriching and visualizing spend data to support category and sourcing decisions.
Category management.It groups similar spend into categories and sets a strategy for each. Sourcing then carries that strategy out.
Strategic sourcing.It runs the RFx events and evaluates bids on several factors, including detailed cost breakdowns.
Contract management.It turns the award into a signed agreement, then tracks obligations and renewals against it.

KPMG’s March 2026 survey found logistics and transportation costs the biggest source of value leakage, at 38%. Inefficient sourcing or suboptimal supplier terms followed at 34%. The survey covered 462 US supply chain leaders at companies with $1 billion or more in annual revenue.

Source to contract steps: from need to signed contract

This guide splits the source to contract process into 5 steps. Each step cuts the supplier list until a single supplier holds a signed agreement.

1
Analyze spend and define the need.

Review what you buy today and what the business requires.

2
Research the market and shortlist suppliers.

Screen for capability, cost and risk.

3
Run the RFx and compare bids.

Issue an RFI, RFP or RFQ and score every response on the same criteria.

4
Negotiate and select.

Agree price, service levels and terms with the preferred supplier.

5
Sign and manage the contract.

Store the agreement, then track obligations and renewals.

What is source to pay (S2P)?

Source to pay (S2P) is the full procurement lifecycle. It includes source to contract and procure to pay, run as a single process. Gartner (page updated July 2026) defines the S2P suite market as an integrated set of solutions to source, contract, request, procure, receive and pay.

S2P is the lifecycle that contains the other 2 processes, not a third process beside them. Supplier, price and contract data entered in sourcing is the same data that purchasing and invoicing need later.

What is procure to pay (P2P)?

Procure to pay (P2P) covers the buying cycle from requisition to supplier payment, under the contract wherever one exists. For contracted spend, it starts once the contract is in place. P2P has 4 activity areas:

eProcurement.Buyers order from catalogs linked to the contract, so purchases match the negotiated terms.
Supply chain collaboration.Buyers and suppliers share order and delivery information.
Invoicing.Software matches each supplier invoice against the purchase order and receipt. People handle the exceptions.
Payments.Approved invoices pay on the agreed terms and post to the ERP.

Hackett’s 2025 Accounts Payable Digital World Class Matrix found that companies with 30%+ touchless invoice processing have, on average, 3.5 times higher AP productivity. The Hackett Group published this provider-perspective study of 15 AP software providers in November 2025. It draws on vendor briefings, Hackett ratings and customer feedback, and it concerns accounts payable, the invoice-to-pay side of P2P.

Key differences between source to pay and procure to pay

The difference between source to pay and procure to pay is where each starts: S2P with the need, P2P with the purchase request. That start point leads to 5 more differences.

1
Scope.

S2P covers sourcing, contracting, purchasing and payment. P2P covers purchasing and payment.

2
Decisions vs action.

S2P decides which suppliers and terms apply. P2P checks that each purchase follows them.

3
Contract management.

In S2P it is a core activity. In P2P the contract already exists, and purchasing works from its terms.

4
Spend visibility.

S2P sees spend before you commit it. P2P sees it as orders and invoices arrive.

5
Where savings come from.

S2P saves on price, terms and supplier choice. P2P saves on errors, cycle time and off-contract buying.

Neither replaces the other. S2C sets the terms, and P2P buys under them.

Where source to contract hands off to procure to pay

The handoff is where the signed contract from source to contract starts to govern purchases in procure to pay. In a strong P2P process, employees buy from approved suppliers at contracted prices.

Which should you start with?

Start with the half where the trouble sits, and plan for both.

If approvals stall, invoices fail to match or purchases skip the contract, start with P2P.
If a few suppliers hold most of your spend, or contracts renew without review, start with source to contract.
If the 2 halves run in separate systems, connect them before you add tools to either.

The Hackett Group (September 2026) names “throwing apps at the problem” as a roadblock to choosing S2C technology. Map the whole process first, then choose the pieces.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between source to pay and procure to pay? +
Source to pay covers the full lifecycle; procure to pay covers only the purchasing half. S2P runs from spend analysis and sourcing through payment. P2P runs from requisition to payment, under the contract where one exists. S2P includes P2P, and P2P does not include strategic sourcing.
What is the difference between source to contract and procure to pay? +
Source to contract sets the terms; procure to pay buys and pays under them. S2C ends in a signed contract that it then manages. P2P enforces that contract’s prices and terms at each purchase.
Where does source to contract end and procure to pay begin? +
The line is the signed contract. Source to contract produces and manages the agreement. P2P starts when someone raises a requisition under it. Contract management continues after signature, so the 2 overlap in practice.
Is contract lifecycle management part of source to contract? +
Yes. Contract lifecycle management is the contract management area of S2C. It runs from drafting and negotiation through signing, monitoring and renewal. The Hackett Group calls it the final concrete step of S2C.
What is the difference between purchase to pay and procure to pay? +
There is no difference in scope. Both names describe the same cycle, from requisition to payment.
How is procure to pay different from accounts payable automation? +
P2P is wider in scope. Accounts payable automation starts when an invoice lands and runs through matching and payment. P2P also covers the steps before that: requisitions, approvals, purchase orders and receiving.
